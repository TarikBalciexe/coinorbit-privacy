CoinOrbit — Data Deletion Request

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data associated with your CoinOrbit account. This page explains how to request data deletion and what data will be affected.

How to Request Data Deletion

1
Send an email to privacy@coinorbit.app with the subject line "Data Deletion Request"
2
Include the email address associated with your CoinOrbit account
3
We will verify your identity and process your request within 30 days
4
You will receive a confirmation email once your data has been deleted

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Account information (email, display name, profile)
  • Portfolio holdings and transaction history
  • Price alert configurations
  • Follow/unfollow preferences
  • Notification preferences and FCM tokens
  • Usage analytics linked to your account

Data That May Be Retained

  • Community signals you published — These may be retained in anonymized form (with your username removed) to maintain leaderboard integrity and historical accuracy
  • Aggregated analytics — Non-identifiable, aggregated usage statistics
  • Legal obligations — Data required to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce agreements (retained for up to 90 days after deletion)

Automatic Data Deletion

If you simply uninstall the app without requesting account deletion, locally stored data (portfolio, alerts, preferences) is automatically removed from your device. Server-side account data will be retained until you request deletion.

Contact: privacy@coinorbit.app
Response time: Within 30 days of receiving your request
