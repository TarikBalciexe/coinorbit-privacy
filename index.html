Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 11, 2026

CoinOrbit ("we", "our", or "us") operates the CoinOrbit mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

Summary: We collect minimal data necessary to provide our services. We do not sell your personal data. We use industry-standard advertising and analytics services.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Account information (email address, display name) when you create an account
  • Portfolio data (coins, quantities, purchase prices) that you manually enter
  • Price alert configurations
  • Trading signals you publish in the community
  • Follow/unfollow preferences for other traders

Information collected automatically:

  • Device information (device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers)
  • Usage data (features accessed, time spent, interaction patterns)
  • Crash reports and performance data
  • IP address and approximate location (country/region level)
  • Advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the App's functionality
  • To send price alerts, signal notifications, and push notifications you've opted into
  • To display your trading signals and profile to other community members
  • To calculate and display leaderboard rankings
  • To process in-app purchases and manage subscriptions
  • To display relevant advertisements
  • To analyze usage patterns and improve user experience
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security issues

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect information:

Google AdMob — We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. Google Privacy Policy

Meta (Facebook) SDK — We use the Meta SDK for advertising attribution and analytics. Meta may collect device information and app usage data. Meta Privacy Policy

Firebase (Google) — We use Firebase for push notifications (FCM), crash reporting (Crashlytics), and analytics. Firebase Privacy

Google Play Billing — Subscription purchases are processed through Google Play. We do not store payment card information. Google Payments Privacy

Binance API — We connect to Binance's public WebSocket API for real-time cryptocurrency price data. No personal data is shared with Binance.

4. Advertising

Our App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. You may see personalized ads based on your interests or non-personalized ads based on contextual information. You can manage your ad preferences through your device's settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization

We participate in the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework and respect your choices regarding ad personalization under GDPR and CCPA.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your portfolio data and alert configurations are stored locally on your device using encrypted storage. Account data and community signals are stored on our secure servers.

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (TLS/SSL) and encryption at rest. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to provide our services. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us. Community signals you published may be retained in anonymized form for leaderboard integrity.

7. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us immediately.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Opt-out: Opt out of personalized advertising
  • Withdraw consent: Where processing is based on consent

For GDPR (EU/EEA), CCPA (California), KVKK (Turkey), and other applicable privacy regulations, please contact us to exercise your rights.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the App or by updating the "Last updated" date. Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
